<b>Apparatus</b><br>
A.	50 ml round bottom flask and a beaker
B.	A dropper 
C.	A magnetic stirrer with a heater
D.	A magnetic bead
E.	A condenser for reflux
<br>
<br>

<b>Chemicals</b><br>
A.	0.5g phthalic anhydride
B.	0.75 g of resorcinol.
C.	Conc. H2SO4
D.	Distilled water
E.	Ammonia solution (5ml)
<br>
<br>

<b>Procedure </b><br>
A.	Weigh out 0.5g of phthalic anhydride and 0.75g of resorcinol and mix them in a round bottom flask.<br>
B.	Add a few drops of conc. H<sub>2</sub>SO<sub>4</sub> (in fume hood) into the round bottom flask.<br>
C.	The mixture is refluxed at 180<sup>0</sup>C till a viscous liquid appears. A magnetic bead is used to stir the solution.<br>
D.	After formation of viscous liquid, it is allowed to cool for some time. <br>
E.	10 ml of distilled water is added and a green fluorescent compound appears. <br>
F.	5 ml of ammonia solution is added into it and stirred. <br>
G.	The mixture is the poured in distilled water, resulting a yellow green solution.<br>
H.	After column chromatography we got a dark red pure solid fluorescein.<br> <br>

<b>Results</b><br>
After column chromatography of the resulted solution a dark red solid product is obtained. The mass of the resulted product is 0.70g.<br><br>

<b>Yield calculation:</b><br>
1 mol of phthalic anhydride reacts to give 1 mol of fluorescein.
So, 148.1g of phthalic anhydride reacts to give 332.31g of fluorescein.
0.5g of phthalic anhydride should give 1.122g of fluorescein if the yield is 100%. <br>

For 0.7g of product the yield is (0.7/1.122)×100 ≈62%.<br>

The absorption peak is observed at 490 nm and the emission maxima is around 521 nm in ethanolic solution of fluorescein, which matches with the reported values.<br><br>
